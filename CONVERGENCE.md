## Part 1 — Intel: ECHOES

- Each news story is tagged once per scan; up to 3 echoes are precomputed then. An echo needs a score of at least 3 *and* either a shared specific motif or two shared motifs. Orbs alone, “official” alone, or a passing mention never qualify, so most stories show no strip.
- Each echo shows year, place, a 192×120 thumbnail and “Same pattern: …”. The strip carries the line “Similar descriptions in history, not a proven link”.
- Tap: 11 light threads and a bloom fire from the tile (transform/opacity only), then the Long Watch entry opens straight onto its record, or the Atlas dossier opens. “◂ Back to transmission” (and closing the Atlas or Long Watch) returns to the same slide.
- Thumbnails load only for the visible slide ±1 (the same window as the scenes) and are released beyond it.

## Part 2 — Atlas: TIME SCRUBBER

- A slider under the map from 1890 to today, with a histogram of records per year and gold notches at each sourced wave. Hub markers ignite when their first record's year arrives, with a flare (emerald for sourced cases, amber for NUFORC submissions) that settles into the normal glow, and fade when you scrub back. Density glows grow with the records reached; constellation links appear only when both hubs are lit.
- Play runs 80 years in 30 s at 1× (0.5×, 1×, 2×, 4×). Tap a decade to jump; drag with momentum; arrow keys, PageUp/PageDown, Home/End. The track owns its gestures, so it never fights map panning.

### Memory across a long session

A full round = Intel (swipe every slide, two echo round trips) → Atlas (play, drag, layers) → Long Watch (whole journey, all six folds). Desktop, four rounds, after GC: JS heap 2.44 → 5.59 → 5.84 → 5.82 → 5.88 MB; DOM nodes 3,164 → 6,020 → 6,073 → 6,073 → 6,073; listeners 310 → 429 → 429 → 429 → 429. The first step is the Long Watch loading once (by design); after that it is flat.

Two leaks found during this testing were already live and are now fixed:
- Atlas: each visit kept its whole loader overlay alive (~110 nodes, 3 listeners) because the loader's mute button registered a window listener that was never removed. `signal-audio.js` now tracks mute buttons weakly.
- Long Watch: each visit kept three preloaded images alive, because clearing an image's src mid-decode pinned it. The preloader now just drops its references.
After the fixes every action measured 0 retained nodes and 0 listeners per repeat (Intel open/close, Atlas open/play/dossier, Long Watch open, entry, Mirror Moment).
